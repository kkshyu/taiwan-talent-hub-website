Getting here

Four ways to arrive

MRT

Ride to Taipei Main Station (Bannan / Tamsui–Xinyi lines), walk through the station-front underground mall toward Zone Z, exit at Z10, and it's diagonally across the street.

TRA / HSR

Arrive at Taipei Main Station, follow signs to the station-front underground mall Zone Z, and leave via Exit Z10. It's about a 3–5 minute walk.

Bus / Coach

Many routes run along Chongqing S. Rd and Zhongxiao W. Rd; alight near Taipei Main Station (Chongqing Rd). Intercity coaches reach Taipei Bus Station, one MRT stop away.

By car / Parking

Street parking around the station is limited. Use navigation to plan your route and park at a nearby off-street car park (such as Taipei Bus Station or Q Square), then walk over.
