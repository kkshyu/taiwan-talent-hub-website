FAQ

What you need to know

What is emoji — Taiwan Talent Hub?

A three-floor space by Taipei Main Station. 1F 在咖啡 (at cafe) and 三點水 (3AM) welcome dining and client meetings without a membership; 2F provides member reading, rest and paid facilities; 3F provides member coworking and an event venue.

Where is it?

No. 11, Section 1, Chongqing South Road, Zhongzheng District, Taipei, diagonally across from Exit Z10 of the Taipei Main Station underground mall.

What are the membership plans and prices?

1F dining needs no membership. A 4-hour day pass is NT$200 and a 12-hour day pass is NT$500, with overtime at NT$100 per hour. Monthly membership is NT$4,000 with 1,000 gift points, quarterly is NT$10,000 with 3,000 points, and annual is NT$35,000 with 10,000 points; all include 2F and 3F access. Founding membership is NT$35,000 for 18 months plus 20,000 gift points valid for one year, limited to 100 members, non-refundable and transferable only.

Is the hub open 24 hours?

The hub uses a split schedule, and 24-hour use of the 2F member space is conditional. Staffed hours are 08:00–17:30 and 18:00–03:00, when 1F dining and 3F coworking and event space are open. From 03:00–08:00 the hub is unstaffed, and members can access 1F and 2F by face recognition.

How do I apply for a community event or enquire about a private event?

Community workshops, talks and meetups can use the 3F venue application. You will receive an application ID, but the venue is not reserved. For company, team or client events and offsites, email us@emoji.tw; timing, price and terms are confirmed separately. Standard 3F private hire is NT$20,000 for 2 hours, with 30 minutes before and after for setup and reset, fully creditable toward same-day 1F food and drink; overtime is NT$5,000 per hour and is not creditable.
